# 📖 おはなし — Stories from the Gomasu world

**ちょっと大げさに、五マスの世界をお届け。**

The twelve pentomino characters have personalities, relationships, opinions, and occasionally scandalous headlines. This section is for **narrative and playful editorial storytelling**, including the census story and 五マス新聞.

## What belongs here?

- Character adventures, interviews, dialogue, and fictional news
- The census, when presented as an entertaining story about the Gomasu world
- Data-driven storytelling when the **narrative** is the main experience

## Editorial promise

**Make a fuss about the facts, not fake the facts.** Sensational headlines and fictional reactions are welcome, but don't invent measured statistics or present fictional claims as real analysis. Make the boundary clear when mixing fictional reporting with actual puzzle data.

**Where does an idea belong?** If the reader comes to enjoy what happens to the characters, it's おはなし. If they come to verify a pattern, see [五マス研究室](../research/README.md). If they come to test a prototype, see [ひみつの実験室](../experimental/README.md).

> 研究室が発見する。新聞社が大騒ぎする。実験室が作ってみる。😂
