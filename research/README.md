# 🔍 五マス研究室 — Research & discoveries

**たった５マス。ふしぎは、まだまだいっぱい。**

This section explores the mathematics, patterns, and surprising discoveries hiding in pentomino puzzles. Our tools are questions, data, careful definitions, and interactive explanations.

## What belongs here?

- Reproducible analyses of puzzle solutions and character arrangements
- Interactive, mobile-first data stories with visual explanations
- Investigations such as [五マスのにじ: combinations vs permutations](https://github.com/chichacha/psychic-pento-guacamole/issues/50)

## Research promise

1. State the question and source data.
2. Document assumptions, selection rules, and methods.
3. Distinguish theoretical possibilities from observed results.
4. Validate counts and acknowledge limitations.
5. Make the discovery understandable and enjoyable, not needlessly academic.

For lightweight publishing, prefer precomputed CSV/JSON with Quarto, vanilla JavaScript, and CSS where practical. Don't publish hypothetical results as measured findings.
